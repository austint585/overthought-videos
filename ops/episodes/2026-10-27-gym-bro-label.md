# Gym bro reads the label

- **id:** 2026-10-27-gym-bro-label
- **Source idea:** Austin's DM, Oct 27 evening (https://www.instagram.com/p/De7YPbrvbJN/, @realriderjet). A gym bro reads a supplement can, shrugs at a crude made-up ingredient, then panics at 0.1 g of sugar. Austin asked for it as a video idea "only if you don't bowdlerize it": the joke is that gym bros will eat anything except sugar.
- **Format:** 3D voxel deadpan explainer in the George apartment, with a kitchen counter, an ingredients card and a sugar comparison.
- **Reference:** a short sketch with on-screen text and no narration worth transcribing. Same beats in the same order: calm through the weird ingredients, a hard stop at the sugar line. The edge stays: we use the real internet rumor that taurine comes from bull sperm, which many of the reel's own commenters bring up, then the fact that it doesn't.

## Script (render_tap markup)

1. A *gym bro* reads the *ingredients.*
2. *Creatine.* Fine. Raw *eggs,* like *Rocky.* *Fine.*
3. *Beef liver,* uncooked. *Delicious.*
4. *Taurine.* Named after the Latin word for *bull.*
5. The internet says it comes from *bull sperm.* He read that. Still *drinking* it.
6. It doesn't, for the record. It was first found in *ox bile* in *1827,* and now it's made in a *lab.*
7. Then he sees it. *Point one grams* of *sugar.*
8. *Absolutely not.* That's *poison.*
9. Meanwhile a *Red Bull,* the *taurine* drink, has about *27* grams.
10. The *gains* are *safe.*

"read" is forced to the past tense with `{/ɹˈɛd/}`.

## Assumptions, written out

- The can he reads is a made-up drink with 0.1 g of sugar, the reference's own joke number. It is not a real product.
- Raw liver and raw eggs are things gym culture is known for, shown as his choices, not as advice.
- "Sugar is poison" is his line, played as the joke.
