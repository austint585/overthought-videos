## Script (`SCRIPT` in `pipeline/render_fri13.py`)

1. It's *Friday the 13th,* and a black cat just crossed his path.
2. Up to 21 million Americans fear this day. There's a word for it, and nobody can *say* it.
3. In 1993, British doctors checked. Hospital admissions from car crashes went *up.*
4. Dutch insurers checked too. *Fewer* crashes. Probably because fewer people *drove.*
5. So the day is only dangerous if you *don't believe in it.*
6. And 2026 has *three* of them. The most a year can get.
7. He survived the first two. He is not leaving the *house.*
8. Send this to the friend who walks under ladders.

Loop: the last shot is the hook framing again and the cat walks past again.

## Facts and sources

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Up to 17 to 21 million Americans have some fear of Friday the 13th | Folklore historian Donald Dossey's estimate, NPR 2008: https://www.npr.org/2008/06/13/91461325/friday-the-13th-the-fear-that-will-not-die . It is an expert estimate, not survey data, so the line says "up to". |
| The fear has a name: paraskevidekatriaphobia (also friggatriskaidekaphobia) | Same NPR piece; HuffPost: https://www.huffingtonpost.com/2017/10/13/friday-the-13th-friggatriskaidekaphobia_n_18258806.html |
| 1993: British doctors found more hospital admissions from transport accidents on Friday the 13th (65 vs 45 on the previous Friday), up to 52% more risk | Scanlon et al., "Is Friday the 13th bad for your health?", BMJ 1993;307:1584: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1697765 . The authors themselves say the numbers are small; the on-screen pill says "small study". |
| Dutch insurers found fewer accident reports on Friday the 13th (about 7,500 vs 7,800 on an average Friday) | Dutch Centre for Insurance Statistics, June 2008, via https://www.dutchnews.nl/2008/06/fewer_accidents_on_friday_13/ and https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/ca/news/home/numbers-prove-friday-13th-not-that-unlucky-47243.aspx |
| "Probably because fewer people drove" | A later Dutch insurance study cited by Insurance Business put the drop down to lower traffic volume on the date (same link). Said as "probably", since it is an explanation, not a measurement. |
| 2026 has three Friday the 13ths (Feb, Mar, Nov), the most a year can have | EarthSky: https://earthsky.org/human-world/years-that-have-3-friday-the-13ths/ ; calendar check: Jan 1 2026 is a Thursday, so Feb 13, Mar 13 and Nov 13 all land on Fridays. |
