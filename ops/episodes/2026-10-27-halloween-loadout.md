# Halloween party loadout

- **id:** 2026-10-27-halloween-loadout
- **Source idea:** Austin's DM, Oct 27 evening (https://www.instagram.com/p/De2HqoExx7g/, @brodyy.up). A guy shows up to a Halloween party with a full "enhancement" kit listed in on-screen text: a size S shirt, height boosters, shoulder pads, concealer, a bulge enhancer, a fake Porsche key and colored contacts. Austin asked for a video about it, with the polo shirt worked in and a nod to the "too many people on that horse" joke about U.S. Polo Assn. (its logo has two riders).
- **Format:** 3D voxel deadpan explainer, in the George apartment set, with a stats panel that climbs item by item.
- **Reference transcript:** the reference is on-screen text over a silent pose, no narration, so there was nothing to transcribe beyond the item list in its caption. We kept the same items in the same order and wrote every line ourselves.
- **Length:** about 44.7 s.

## Assumptions, written out

- "Three-inch insoles" picks the top of the usual 1 to 3 inch range; 5 ft 9 plus 3 in is the 6 ft 0 on screen.
- The 1984 suit was filed by USPA first (asking a court to say its logo was fine), and Ralph Lauren countersued. "In court over it since 1984" covers that fight and its later rounds; it does not claim one continuous trial.
- FRAME %, ITEMS and AURA in the stats panel are the joke, not measurements.
- The horse drawings are our own crude silhouettes, not either company's logo art.
- The guy is a made-up voxel figure, not the creator of the reference reel.
