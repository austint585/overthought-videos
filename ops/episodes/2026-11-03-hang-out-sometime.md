- **id:** 2026-11-03-hang-out-sometime
- **Source idea:** ours. A relatable friendship joke people send to one specific friend: the one who always says "we should hang out sometime" and never does. The real 2018 University of Kansas study on how many hours it takes to make a friend does the math.
- **Format:** pastel toy joke explainer, calm Chatterbox read (exaggeration 0.5), dark caption pills with one boxed word. Cast: two toy mugs with faces (you and the friend). Odd detail: the mugs grow old. ## Assumptions and jokes (not claims)

- "Twice a year, for two hours each" is our example, said as "say you two really hang out", and the on-screen note says it is an example, not data.
- 4 hours a year to reach 200 hours is 50 years, plain arithmetic; it treats the study's hours as cumulative milestones, which is how KU's release presents them. The porch note says "200 hours at 4 hours a year".
- "It counts as zero hours" (sending a reel) is a joke. The study counted time spent together.
- The year counter (2026 to 2076) and the old mugs are gags.
