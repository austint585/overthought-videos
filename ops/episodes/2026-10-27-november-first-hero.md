# The November 1st pharmacy hero

- **id:** 2026-10-27-november-first-hero
- **Source idea:** Austin's DM, Oct 27 evening (https://www.instagram.com/p/DeqIPOLsTSH/, @nick2flyy__): a guy in a superhero cape walks a drugstore aisle on November 1st "to save and meet" the love of his life. Austin: "this is actually a good november first one that is funny", and asked to work in "Michael Crackson's Halloween party". It also folds in his earlier idea (ops/episodes/2026-10-27-cvs-charge.md): the clueless boyfriend and the $50 morning-after pharmacy charge, with our own punchline.
