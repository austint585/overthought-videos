## Assumptions, written out

- I left out the "100 deaths a year from pen caps" number that many articles repeat: I could not trace it to a primary source.
- Ball size and ISO standard are for the BIC Cristal. "Your" pen may differ; the on-screen note names the Cristal.
- The windpipe cutaway is schematic and carries an on-screen note: the hole buys time, and it is still an emergency.
- "Someone saw you coming" is a joke, not a suggestion that chewing is safe.
- The visuals show a generic clear stick pen with no logo.
