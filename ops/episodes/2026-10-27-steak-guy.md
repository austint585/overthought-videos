# The 8 a.m. Steak Guy

- **id:** 2026-10-27-steak-guy
- **Source idea:** Austin's DM, Oct 27 (https://www.instagram.com/p/De-VQ7qR3-n/, @_nofoodwasted_, 60 s). A student eats a whole steak on the bone with a bottle of ranch in an 8 a.m. lecture; the clip has no narration. Austin wants it done as a low-poly animation with a dry narrator and a lot of wit.
- **Format:** 3D voxel brainrot explainer (the spawner kit, moved into a lecture hall), with a nichelarp-style counter panel top-left (here: an AURA score).
- **Length:** 35 s.

## Script (render_tap markup)

1. The guy eating a *steak* in the back of your *8{eight} a.m.* has the most *aura* on campus.
2. He walks in late holding a *tomahawk ribeye.* *36{thirty-six} ounces,* sold as a steak for *sharing.*
3. He is not *sharing.*
4. No plate. No fork. The *7{seven} inch bone* is the *handle.*
5. Then he opens a full bottle of *ranch,* and the row in front of him starts *buffering.*
6. The USDA says steak is safe at *145{one forty-five} degrees* after a *3{three} minute* rest. He rested it during *attendance.*
7. The professor makes eye contact. He keeps *chewing.*
8. Scientists call this *main character energy.* He calls it *breakfast.*

All lines are ours. The original clip has no script or punchline to copy; the character is a generic blocky student, not the person in the clip.

## Assumptions, written out

- **Aura points are the joke, not a measurement.** The counter (+500, +1,000, +1,500, +2,000, +2,500, +10,000, total 17,500) is a made-up score, the same way "aura" is used as slang. No real-world number depends on it.
- The steak is drawn larger than real scale so it reads on a phone. It is a cartoon, and nothing on screen claims a size beyond the sourced 36 oz and 7 in.
- "Walks in late", "full bottle", "row in front starts buffering", "rested it during attendance" are the fictional scene, not claims.
- The professor's glasses render as a dark bar (shades look). Fine for a cartoon.
