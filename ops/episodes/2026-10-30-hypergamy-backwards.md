# Hypergamy, but backwards

- **id:** 2026-10-30-hypergamy-backwards
- **Source idea:** Austin's DM, Oct 29 (@lowpolyvid reel, link in the DM thread): what if hypergamy went the other way around, a role-reversal satire. Our own script and picture.
- **Format:** clay deadpan short (Kokoro, calm), 40.6 s. New set: a staircase of six landings at night, each its own place (marble mansion door, cubicle, podcast corner, mall vape kiosk, parking lot, dumpster). Frame one shows the whole staircase. Follows the rotation: the last two queued were an object explainer and a body macro.
- **Files:** `pipeline/render_hypo.py`, `pipeline/hypo_scene.py`.

## Script (render_tap markup)

1. Hypergamy has an *opposite.* It's called *hypogamy.* Dating *down.*
2. She starts at the top. A *neurosurgeon.* Full head of hair. Owns his *house.*
3. *Disgusting.* She gives him two weeks' *notice.*
4. Step two. A *regional manager.* He still has a *401(k).* She can smell it on him.
5. Step three. A guy with a *podcast.* Four listeners. One is his *mom.*
6. Better. But he owns a *bed frame.*
7. Step four. He sells *vapes* at the mall. He calls the kiosk his *office.*
8. Step five. He lives in a *Honda Civic.* He owes her *forty dollars.*
9. She has never felt more *alive.*
10. Her friends all tell her the same thing. *You could do so much worse.*
11. So she *does.*

## Assumptions, written out

- Only the word is presented as fact. An on-screen note says so at the end: "Hypogamy is a real word. The rest is a joke."
- Sociologists do not all use the terms the same way (HowStuffWorks notes one definition framed around men marrying down). The script uses the common sense, dating down.
- I left out the "62 percent of mixed-education marriages" figure: I could only find it second hand and could not open The Atlantic piece.
- Net worth numbers, her interest bar, the listeners and the characters are invented and read as the joke. No real people.
- Pronounced hy-POG-uh-mee, set by hand in LEXICON. "Lives" is forced to the verb reading.
