# We Could Go Up (our own version, told by a cat)

- **Source idea:** Austin, Nov 12 DM after the raw liver Reel: "make your own version of 'we could go up'". The raw liver cut used another man's words. This one is our own speech in the @cluberich edit's form.
- **The original:** @cluberich, "Or we could go up.." (https://www.tiktok.com/@cluberich/video/7705057053185723670), Nov 6, 5.3M plays. A man's hype speech, 45 s, over a slow dark bed at 107.7 BPM, with white line-art graphics on black and 6 s of grainy luxury-car B-roll on the drop. Its beats, in order:
  1. the hook ("we could stay here, or we could go up");
  2. a "do you not understand" jab;
  3. "are you not tired of" the same life;
  4. three "it's not tomorrow / next week / next month" lines, then "you have to start now";
  5. "Why?";
  6. three "there's always going to be" obstacles;
  7. "You know what's okay? Failing";
  8. "But you know what's not okay? Not trying".
- **Our take:** the same beats in the same order, but it's a cat's pep talk to itself before the kitchen counter. "Go up" is literal (cats always want the highest point). The obstacles are the glass, the plant and someone yelling its full name. Failing becomes missing the jump, and you land on your feet anyway. The last line turns "not trying" into the thing every cat owner knows: leaving the glass on the counter.
- **Why people send it:** every cat owner has watched this happen. "Send this to someone whose cat does this" is the share. It also works as a parody for the millions who know the sound.
- **Words:** our own. The one exception is the hook, "or we could go up", which Austin named as the thing to make our version of. No other line or sign-off is lifted from the original.
- **Voice:** the designed hype voice (`qwen_hype`, Qwen3-TTS, designed from a text description, not a clone of anyone). Austin said the raw liver cut sounded choppy (see below for what changed).
- **Music:** the TikTok's own bed, the same Demucs split used in the raw liver cut (`pipeline/out/ref/cluberich/split/`, never committed). Its drop lands on our payoff cut, and the video ends where the song ends. Same risk as before: Instagram may match it and mute or limit the Reel. Austin took that risk on the raw liver cut.
- **Facts:** none are claimed. It is a joke speech, and the cat behaviors (climbing to the highest spot, knocking things off counters, the righting reflex) are everyday ones, not numbers.

## Why the raw liver voice sounded choppy, and the fix

Measured from `pipeline/out/liver/narration*.json`:
- Every sentence was its own take, so each one restarted its intonation.
- voice_fix then stretched each line toward the median pace by a different amount: 1.04, 1.10, 0.91, 1.04, 0.93, 0.97. Neighbouring lines swung by up to 19%.
- Every gap came out at 0.36 to 0.40 s, like a metronome. In the meme clip, the answer follows its question in 0.14 to 0.18 s.

This episode:
- It is voiced in breath groups: a line here is a whole passage of one to four sentences, so the voice carries through it.
- voice_fix runs with stretching capped at 4% and the takes' own pauses kept.
- Each gap between passages is set by hand from the original's rhythm.

## Script (8 passages, one take each)

1. We could stay down here... or we could go up.
2. Do you not see that counter?
3. Are you not tired of staring at the bottom of the table, while they eat chicken up there?
4. So you jump. Not when they leave for work. Not when they fall asleep. Now.
5. Why? Because there's always going to be a glass in the way. A plant in the way. Somebody yelling your full name.
6. You know what's okay? Missing. Missing is okay. You land on your feet anyway.
7. But you know what's not okay?
8. Leaving that glass on the counter.
9. (music only, on the song's drop) the payoff: the glass bursts on the floor, then the cat is shot on top of the fridge like a car commercial.

Caption: "Every cat owner knows how this ends. #cats #catsoftiktok #motivation"

Scroll patrol (`2026-11-13-cat-go-up-patrol.md`) scored the opening 5 of 10 and the fixed one 8 of 10. Changes taken from it:
- frame 0 moves and has words on it;
- the caption promises the ending instead of repeating the first line;
- the music-only payoff is 4.2 s with a fade, not 6 s to the song's end.
