## Assumptions and jokes (not claims)

- "Your friend who deep cleaned his apartment", "the guy next door", "robot girlfriend", "the fit", "next season" as a dating metaphor: framing, not claims.
- "Come on too strong, and she leaves": a simplification of the 2002 finding (successful males adjust intensity to the female's startle response).
- "Most guys get nobody": the skew is real; "nobody" is a joke rounding of "few or no matings in a season".
- Not claimed: how many bowers a female visits (studies disagree, so the script leaves it out).
- Pictured behaviors are stylized clay; the bird proportions are not exact. End pill: "ALL REAL. SATIN BOWERBIRD, EASTERN AUSTRALIA."
