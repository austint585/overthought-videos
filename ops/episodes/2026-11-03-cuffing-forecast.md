# Cuffing season forecast

- **id:** 2026-11-03-cuffing-forecast
- **Source idea:** ours, topical (first week of November). The relatable friend joke our numbers favor: the friend who is single all summer and suddenly has a girlfriend in November. Played as a deadpan TV weather forecast where every number is real.
- **Format:** toon joke forecast, calm Chatterbox read (exaggeration 0.5). New sets: a weather studio with a map and anchor desk, a lab with a "hot-o-meter", a breakup chart, a Christmas tree, a giant phone, a shelf of birthday cakes, a thaw. New cast: a thermometer weatherman whose red column drops in the cold front, teeth chattering (the one odd physical detail), sunglasses at the end. Follows the rotation: the last queued episode was a clay nature short (Bowerbird) and before it a pastel toy explainer (AI water).

## Assumptions and jokes (not claims)

- "That's not love. That's weather.", the cold front, the weather warning, "forecast for summer: single": framing.
- "Nine months after the holidays": the timing is our framing; the script states only where the birthdays fall, not why.
- "Clears up around Valentine's Day": the common folk definition of the season's end, said as a forecast, not as data.
- The breakup chart bars are an illustration of the 2010 chart's shape (rise through November, peak around Dec 11, drop at Christmas), not plotted values. The study counted status updates, not actual breakups.
- The "body photo" is shown as an abstract pink hourglass on an easel. The "hot-o-meter" and its MEH and HOTTER marks are a joke dial, not the study's scale.
- Sources pill on the last shot: "Data: Pawlowski & Sorokowski 2008, McCandless & Byron 2010, Hinge, SSA births 1994-2014".
