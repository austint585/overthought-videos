## Facts and sources

| Claim on screen | Source |
|---|---|
| Turkey's tryptophan is about the same as chicken or beef | Harvard Health Publishing, Robert H. Shmerling MD, "The turkey coma: fact or fiction?" (updated Apr 24 2026): "similar to the amount in chicken or beef". https://www.health.harvard.edu/diet-and-nutrition/the-turkey-coma-fact-or-fiction |
| The drowsiness is from digesting a big meal | Same Harvard page: "it's more likely just from digesting a big meal." Scientific American (Jason Kane, PBS NewsHour, Nov 23 2011): turkey's tryptophan "is comparable to that contained in many other meats", and the drowsiness "probably results from carbs and alcohol". https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/eating-turkey-sleepy-thanksgiving/ Texas A&M (Matt Hoffman DNP, Nov 21 2018): "The real culprit is usually the overwhelming carbohydrate intake." https://stories.tamu.edu/news/2018/11/21/does-turkey-make-you-sleepy/ |
