- **id:** 2026-11-04-36-questions
- **Source idea:** ours. A dating joke people send to one friend: the one who takes a viral "fall in love" trick far too seriously. The trick is real: Arthur Aron's 1997 closeness study (36 questions) and the "four minutes of eye contact" ending made famous by the 2015 NYT Modern Love essay.

## Assumptions and jokes (not claims)

- The friend, the date, the fries, the exit and the waiter are all fiction. "Laminated" and "They're engaged" are jokes.
- We do not claim the questions make anyone fall in love. The script only says pairs felt closer than small-talk pairs, which is what the study measured.
- We do not say the stare was in the 1997 lab study (sources disagree on that); the script calls it "the viral version".
- The question counter (1 of 36 up to 36 of 36) and the stopwatch are decoration.
