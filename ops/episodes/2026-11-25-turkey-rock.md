2. The fridge needs about a day for every *four to five pounds.*
3. You don't have *four days.*
4. So it goes in *cold water.* About thirty minutes a pound.
5. A *20*{twenty} pound bird takes about *ten hours.*
6. And you change the water every *thirty minutes.*
7. That's *20*{twenty} water changes.

## Facts and sources

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Fridge thaw: about one day (24 hours) for every 4 to 5 pounds | USDA, "Thawing your turkey", https://www.usda.gov/node/20073 ; Nebraska Extension, https://food.unl.edu/how-thaw-turkey |
| A 20 lb bird needs 4 to 5 days in the fridge ("you don't have four days") | 20 / 5 = 4 and 20 / 4 = 5 days; UGA Extension chart lists 4 to 5 days for 16 to 20 lb, https://fieldreport.caes.uga.edu/publications/C1226/ |
| Cold water: about 30 minutes per pound, bird in a leak-proof bag, water changed every 30 minutes, cook right after | USDA FSIS, https://www.fsis.usda.gov/es/node/3299 ; FSIS "Turkey Basics: Safe Thawing" (archived), https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20120915114721/http:/www.fsis.usda.gov/PDF/Turkey_Basics_Safe_Thawing.pdf ; Illinois Extension, https://extension.illinois.edu/turkey-site/thawing |
| A 20 lb bird takes about 10 hours in cold water | 20 x 30 min = 600 min = 10 h; the FSIS chart gives 8 to 10 hours for 16 to 20 lb |
| 20 water changes | 10 h / 30 min = 20 |

Assumptions written out: the bird is 20 lb (a common big Thanksgiving bird, and the top of the USDA chart rows quoted). "About" stays in every line, as in the sources. The bag is on the bird in every shot, and the water is never warm. "Your friend is on number one" is the joke, not a claim.

Honesty notes on screen: fridge shot "USDA: about 1 day per 4 to 5 lb in the fridge"; sink shots "USDA: about 30 min per lb, in a sealed bag, water changed every 30 min"; count "Our math: 10 h / 30 min = 20".
