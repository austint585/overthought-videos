# Schrödinger's situationship (2026-10-27)

**Idea.** From Austin's DM of a "Schrödinger's" reel (Oct 26). We kept the physics-as-dating-advice angle but changed the subject: a situationship is "dating" and "not dating" at once until someone asks what you are. No slur, nothing sexual.

**Format.** 3D voxel brainrot explainer (voxel kit). A shipping crate with its front sawn off; inside, a couch with a couple shown as two ghost states ("DATING" and "NOT"), and the cat alive and dead. A friend with a flashlight opens the box and the states collapse.

**Files.** `pipeline/render_quantum.py`, `quantum_scene.py`, `quantum_anim.py`.

## Script

1. Every *situationship* is secretly a *quantum physics* problem.
2. In *1935,* Erwin Schrödinger imagined a cat in a sealed box that's *alive and dead* at once, until somebody *looks.*
3. A situationship follows the same rules. Until someone asks *what are we,* you're *dating* and *not dating.*
4. You text every morning. *Dating.* You've never met their friends. *Not dating.*
5. Physicists call this *superposition.* The group chat calls it *delulu.*
6. And it stays stable as long as *nobody measures it.*
7. Then a friend asks, *so what are you two?* And the whole thing *collapses.*
8. In the original setup, the odds were *fifty-fifty.* Schrödinger called it a *ridiculous case.*
9. He was *right.*

## Facts and sources

- **1935, cat in a sealed box, alive and dead until observed.** Schrödinger, "Die gegenwärtige Situation in der Quantenmechanik", *Die Naturwissenschaften*, 1935 (three parts). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schr%C3%B6dinger%27s_cat
- **Fifty-fifty.** In the original setup, within the hour "one of the atoms will perhaps decay, but also, with equal probability, that none of them will" (Wikipedia's quote of the paper). Same page.
- **"Ridiculous case."** Trimmer's English translation (Proceedings of the American Philosophical Society, vol. 124, no. 5, 1980, pp. 323-338) opens the passage with "One can even set up quite ridiculous cases." The German word is *burlesk*; Wikipedia's own translation says "burlesque". https://personal.lse.ac.uk/robert49/teaching/partiii/pdf/SchroedingerPresentSituation1935(1980trans).pdf and https://arxiv.org/pdf/1308.5290
- **Superposition.** The box is described by one wave function combining the alive and dead outcomes until it is observed (same Wikipedia page).
- **"Situationship."** On Oxford Languages' Word of the Year 2023 shortlist (the winner was "rizz"). Not claimed in the script.

## Assumptions

- "Measuring" a relationship by asking "what are we" is the joke's analogy, not physics; the script presents it as a joke ("follows the same rules", "the group chat calls it delulu").
- Schrödinger meant the case as a criticism of the Copenhagen reading; "He was right" is a joke on his word "ridiculous", not a claim about the physics debate.
- Schrödinger is a historical figure cited for his published thought experiment. He is not shown or impersonated.
