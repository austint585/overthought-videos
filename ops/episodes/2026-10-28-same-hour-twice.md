## Script (render_tap markup)

1. This *Sunday* at *2{two} a.m.,* the clock goes back to *1{one} a.m.*
2. You get the same *hour* twice.
3. In *2022{twenty twenty-two},* the *Senate* voted to make daylight time *permanent.* The *House* never voted.
4. This *July,* the *House* passed it, *308{three oh eight} to 117{one seventeen}.* The *Senate* hasn't.
5. Congress agrees on this. Just never in the *same year.*
6. *Hawaii* and most of *Arizona* don't change their clocks at all.
7. A Michigan study found heart attacks drop about *21{twenty-one} percent* the Tuesday after the fall change.
8. In spring, when we lose the hour, they jump about *25{twenty-five} percent* that Monday.
9. You will spend your free hour *scrolling.*
10. See you in *March.*

## Assumptions, written out

- "Daylight time permanent" is the Sunshine Protection Act; the 2022 Senate bill and the 2026 House bill are different bill numbers of the same act. "The Senate hasn't" means it has not passed the House bill as of Oct 28 2026.
- "Just never in the same year" is the joke and true so far: each chamber has passed it once, in different Congresses.
- The heart attack numbers are from one Michigan study of hospital admissions (2010 to 2013); the script says "a Michigan study".
- Arizona: the Navajo Nation does observe DST, hence "most of Arizona".
- "You will spend your free hour scrolling" is the joke, not a statistic.
