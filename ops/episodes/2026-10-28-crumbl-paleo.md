# The man who took down Crumbl

- **id:** 2026-10-28-crumbl-paleo
- **Source idea:** Austin's DM, Oct 28 (https://www.instagram.com/p/De9jkCuDXcv/, @millionaire.mindset_club, a 171 s 3D "rise and fall of Crumbl" explainer). He asked for our version: what happened to Crumbl, plus the Santa Cruz paleo coach who paid people outside Crumbl not to buy cookies, played as the joke cause ("I know that isn't exactly what did it but for the sake of the joke"), how he made people healthier, and end on what he takes down next.
- **Reference transcript:** watched with vidIQ (10 credits; the free in-browser recorder was blocked by the permission check). It is a straight business breakdown: the 70% sales drop, 57 closures, store growth, three reasons, the franchise royalty model. We keep its real numbers and none of its sentences, and turn it into a deadpan satire short with a fake culprit that reads as the joke.
- **Format:** 3D voxel deadpan short, about 35 s. A strip-mall parking lot at dusk with a pink cookie shop, a paleo coach handing out cash to customers who turn around, the shop going dark, and him looking at the shop next door. Satire, so the calm deadpan voice is right (Austin's screaming-narrator rule is for straight educational reels).

10. Crumbl is *down.* Who does he take out *next?*

- "Every cookie he stopped was about 720 calories": uses the Milk Chocolate Chip figure; flavors vary.
- He is a public figure who posts these videos himself; he appears as a generic voxel figure, no photo or likeness. Crumbl appears as a pink voxel shop with its name on the overlay, as commentary.
