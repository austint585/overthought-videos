# 3D episode plan, Nov 8 to 14 2026

Written by the Saturday planning run on Nov 7. One episode a day for the 12:30 pm ET slot; the make run fills the other hourly slots as usual. Nothing here is rendered or queued: the laptop builds, QA's and queues each one under the standing approval.

Why these seven (from `reports/weekly/2026-11-07.md`): calm tag-a-friend jokes on looks, dating and identity got every share this week (316 of 316), and the calendar-tied ones did best (Cuffing Season 8,684 plays, No Shave 3,803, Turkey Drop 2,036). Yelled straight explainers got 0 shares and under 34% watched, so there are none this week. Each script is 27 to 35 s, opens on the friend doing the thing, has one sourced fact as the turn, and ends on the tag line that runs back into the first line. Two are calendar-locked (Nov 12 Thanksgiving thaw, Nov 13 Friday the 13th) and one is a Part 2 of a top Reel.

Kits alternate toon and pastel toy, starting with toon because the last queued item (The Period Text, Nov 7 10:30) is pastel toy. No new kit is needed for any episode; every one needs a new set (sets and openings are listed so none repeats the episode before it). No DM idea in `ops/ideas.md` is both unmade and fits a kit this week (the role-reversal one became Hypergamy, But Backwards; the weather-app VPN one stays held).

Rules carried over: no em dashes, never "that's not an X, that's a Y", calm Chatterbox read (`exaggeration=0.5`), scroll patrol before building, captions of one or two plain sentences plus 3 hashtags. Every number below has a source; the laptop run re-opens each link before render (`ops/make.md` step 1) and writes the full pack in `ops/episodes/<id>.md`.
