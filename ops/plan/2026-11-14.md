# 3D episode plan, Nov 15 to 21 2026

Written by the Saturday planning run on Nov 14. One episode a day for the 12:30 pm ET slot; the laptop builds, QA's and queues each one under the standing approval. Nothing here is rendered or queued.

Why these five (from `reports/weekly/2026-11-14.md` and the "Pick the idea" order in `ops/make.md`):

- No unmade DM idea in `ops/ideas.md` fits a kit this week (the bicep heart and the go up edit are already made and waiting in the queue). So every episode is a **timely moment** (rule 2) or a **form x line pairing** from the Nov 12 blind test (rule 3). No generic relatable jokes, no sequels, no straight explainers.
- **Thu Nov 19 and Fri Nov 20 are left open on purpose** for same-day remakes of whatever Austin DMs. Trending DMs made the hits; nothing in this plan should push one out.
- Skip over 50% killed reach this week (138 to 225 plays), so each episode opens on the punchline's picture, not a setup. No pause-to-play mechanic and no game-launch joke.
- Every script is 20 to 35 s, calm read, ends on a "send this to the friend who" line, and loops back to its first line where it can. No em dashes, never "that's not an X, that's a Y". Scroll patrol before building (`ops/make.md` step 3).
- Sources were found from the cloud through search results; several sites (knowyourmeme.com, science.nasa.gov, fsis.usda.gov, un.org) do not resolve here, so the laptop run re-opens every link before render and writes the full pack in `ops/episodes/<id>.md`.

Kits used: toon, voxel, pastel toy. No new kit; each needs a new set.

## 1. Sun Nov 15: Sir, This Is A Wendy's

- **id:** 2026-11-15-sir-wendys
- **Kit:** toon (`toon.py`; start from `render_height.py` and `height_scene.py`). New set: a fast-food counter lit like a boxing corner (ring rope along the counter edge, one hard spotlight, a round timer on the menu board).
- **Pairing (blind test idea 2):** the form is an earnest corner-coach motivational speech, as in the cats-boxing reel at 1.5M on Nov 10 (https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfjX_KStX9j). The line is "Sir, this is a Wendy's", the reply people use for someone ranting where nobody asked.
- **Frame 1:** a coach in a towel and headset, mid-scream, gripping a trembling cashier's shoulders across the counter. The round timer reads 0:59 and counts down. On-screen text: "ROUND 12".
- **Sound:** a boxing bell from the Sonniss bundle on frame 1, crowd bed under the speech, the bed cuts to silence for the line.

Script (`render_tap.py` format; the coach's lines, then the cashier):
1. Look at me. They said you'd never *make it*.
2. Too small. Too slow. Too *soft*.
3. Every time you went down, I watched you get back up.
4. Tonight you take what's *yours*.
5. (cashier, flat) Sir, this is a *Wendy's*.
6. (coach, beat) A number six. And the *heart* of a champion.
7. Send this to the friend who gives speeches at the drive-through.

## 2. Mon Nov 16: Fifteen Wishes An Hour (Leonids)

- **id:** 2026-11-16-leonid-wishes
- **Kit:** voxel (`render_spawner.py`, `spawner_scene.py`). New set: a night rooftop on a floating block island, a voxel friend with eyes shut and hands clasped, meteors streaking overhead.
- **Timely:** the Leonids peak the night of Nov 16 to 17. Follows the week's best Reel (11/11, the friend who wishes on everything).
- **Frame 1:** a meteor already crossing the sky, the friend mid-wish. Counter top-left: "WISHES: 1", ticking up with each streak.

Script:
1. Tonight your friend gets up to *fifteen* free wishes an hour.
2. The *Leonids* peak tonight.
3. Each one is a crumb of comet *Tempel-Tuttle*, hitting the air at about 44{forty-four} miles a second.
4. The comet swings by every *33*{thirty-three} years.
5. In 1966{nineteen sixty-six}, the sky dropped about *40*{forty} a second.
6. That's 2,400{twenty-four hundred} wishes a minute.
7. Your friend still wished for the same guy.
8. Send this to the friend who wishes on everything.
