# 3D episode plan, Nov 29 to Dec 5 2026

Written by the Saturday planning run on Nov 28. One episode a day for the 12:30 pm ET slot; the laptop builds, QA's and queues each one under the standing approval. Nothing here is rendered or queued. Do not queue an episode whose date has passed.

Why these (from `reports/weekly/2026-11-28.md` and the "Pick the idea" order in `ops/make.md`):

- No unmade DM idea in `ops/ideas.md` fits a kit (the Nov 22 and 26 DMs are direction and references). So every episode is a **timely moment** (rule 2). DM remakes still come first: **Sat Dec 5 is left open** for one, and any DM that arrives during the week pushes a plan episode back, not the other way round.
- **Saw It On A Reel: Turkey** had 27.6K plays, 90.5% watched and 298 shares. The other three chad episodes all held 62% or more. So five of the six are chad episodes: each is a belief or fact people repeat that week, corrected by the chad with a named source, and ends on "Saw it on a reel." This also follows Austin's Nov 22 direction to pick one niche and make it in the game-cutscene style.
- Tue Dec 1 is a pastel toy fact episode, so the account doesn't run the same look all week (`ops/style_rules.md`: never the same style twice in a row; the make run decides the order with whatever DM remakes land in between).
- No line art (three of three under 200 plays).
- Every episode is the low-poly PS2 cast (`pipeline/render_ps2.py`; Austin, Nov 26: "Yes, rebuild them") and **every one needs a new set in `lowpoly_sets.py`** (`ops/questions.md` question 2 and 3: the place where the topic happens). The existing sets are the patio table, `tv_aisle` and `living_room`; none is reused below. No new kit is needed; a new set is about the cost of `living_room`.
- Cast per episode is only who the joke needs (question 4): never the four friends and their salary tags by default, and no phone search card unless that episode lists one.
- The last three posts were Black Friday (TV aisle, four guys), Full Price (line art) and Rudolph (living room, chad and girlfriend). The first frame of each episode below is new.
- Scripts are 17 to 24 s, the length of the four chad episodes. Hook text on frame 0, no em dashes, never "that's not an X, that's a Y". Scroll patrol before building, and the full `ops/questions.md` answers in each pack.
- Sources were found from the cloud through search results; realchristmastrees.org and axios.com do not resolve here. The laptop run re-opens every link before render and writes the full pack in `ops/episodes/<id>.md`. Numbers marked "check" must be confirmed word for word first, or the line gets cut.

Kits used: low-poly cast (5), pastel toy (1). No new kit.
