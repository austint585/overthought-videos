# 3D episode plan, Nov 22 to 28 2026

Written by the Saturday planning run on Nov 21. One episode a day for the 12:30 pm ET slot; the laptop builds, QA's and queues each one under the standing approval. Nothing here is rendered or queued.

**Posting is paused** (`ops/PAUSE`, Austin, Nov 14, until he says the photos are done). Build these anyway so they are ready, but the dated ones (Thanksgiving week) only work if the pause lifts in time; when it lifts, Sir, This Is A Wendy's (already `ready`) goes first, then whatever in this list is still on its date. Do not queue an episode whose date has passed.

Why these five (from `reports/weekly/2026-11-21.md` and the "Pick the idea" order in `ops/make.md`):

- No unmade DM idea in `ops/ideas.md` fits a kit (the Nov 15 and 16 DMs are references, not remakes). So every episode is a **timely moment** (rule 2) or a **form x line pairing** (rule 3).
- Thanksgiving is Thursday Nov 26 and Black Friday is Nov 27 (Nov 1 2026 is a Sunday, so the fourth Thursday is the 26th). Named moments carried the month's best shares (11/11, 10 shares), so three episodes ride that week.
- Two carry-overs from last week's plan that are still in date: Why Would I Deceive You? and Remember November 2026 (this month only).
- **Tue Nov 24 and Sat Nov 28 are left open** for same-day remakes of whatever Austin DMs.
- Looks: no white line art this week (two of two stalled under 200 plays). Rotate per `ops/style_rules.md`, never the same look twice in a row. The clay bicep heart held viewers best, so clay gets two slots. Voxel only for Minecraft-styled sources, so none here.
- Every script is 20 to 35 s, calm read, opens on the punchline's picture, ends on a "send this to the friend who" line. No em dashes, never "that's not an X, that's a Y". Scroll patrol before building.
- Sources were found from the cloud through search results; fsis.usda.gov, history.com, health.harvard.edu, extension.illinois.edu and knowyourmeme.com do not resolve here. The laptop run re-opens every link before render and writes the full pack in `ops/episodes/<id>.md`.

Kits used: pastel toy, clay, toon. No new kit; each needs a new set.
