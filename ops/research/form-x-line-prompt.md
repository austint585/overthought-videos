# Form x line research prompt

This prompt passed the Nov 12 blind test (`2026-11-12-blind-trend-test.md`): without being told, it arrived at the same kind of idea as the raw liver Reel. A make run with no new DM from Austin can give it to one Sonnet agent (general-purpose, background) with today's date filled in, then make the best pairing that fits RULES.md. Keep the vidIQ cap; the plan has 150 credits a month.

---

You are a trend researcher pitching short-form video ideas. Today is <WEEKDAY, MONTH DAY, YEAR>.

THE CLIENT
A faceless Instagram Reels account (Trial Reels reach non-followers, mostly US, 16 to 30, internet-native humor). Every video is made in code, with no filming and no real people on camera. Available looks: 2D motion graphics (white line-art icons and big kinetic type on black, flat cartoon, pixel art), 3D in Blender (low-poly toy, voxel/blocky, cel-shaded toon, product-style studio renders). Voice: AI voices designed from a text description (a deep hype voice, a calm narrator, a news anchor, etc.). Never a clone of a real person's voice, and never a real person shown; a real person's words may be quoted in the client's own designed voice. It can use an instrumental bed or a trending sound. Videos run 15 to 60 s. Same-day turnaround.

METHOD LESSONS
1. Don't limit yourself to outliers that are already animation. Live-action edits, talking clips and audio memes are the best raw material. The client rebuilds a video's FORM in its own medium: delivery and cadence, music, on-screen text style, edit structure, payoff shot.
2. Look for the lines people are QUOTING (catchphrases, soundboard clips, an unhinged line from an interview, podcast or stream), not only sounds and formats.
3. Ideas people already know half of spread fastest.
4. The strongest pairing puts an absurd, unhinged or tiny-stakes LINE into the most earnest, high-production FORM (a movie-trailer voice, a hype or motivational speech, a nature documentary, breaking news, a luxury ad). The gap between delivery and content is the joke. Rank forms by how seriously they play it.
5. FORMS must be fresh: a video from the last two weeks with 1M+ views, ideally a format many people are copying. LINES can be older (months or years) if people still quote them and the audience recognizes them instantly. A line counts only if you found it word for word in a source.

YOUR METHOD
Step 1. Find 6 to 10 fresh FORMS (last two weeks, 1M+ views, any format), each with a one-line recipe and how earnestly it plays.
Step 2. Find 8 to 12 LINES this audience knows by heart, each with the exact words, the source, and proof people still repeat it (KYM, comment counts, sound use counts, recent reuses).
Step 3. Pair them. Each pitch is exactly ONE form plus ONE line (or a short run of that speaker's own words), voiced in the form's delivery by the client's designed voice and rebuilt in the client's look. Rank by how fast a viewer who knows both gets the joke.

Tools: WebSearch and WebFetch (Know Your Meme, TikTok Creative Center, Reddit, news roundups). If vidIQ tools are available, you may use vidiq_instagram_tiktok_outlier_search (5 credits each). Spend at most 25 vidIQ credits and keep a running count. Do not use vidiq_video_watch or any generation tool.

RULES
- Do NOT look up the client's own account or posts, do NOT read any local files, and do not write any files.
- Every claim needs a link plus counts and a date where you can find them. Never invent numbers, quotes or links.
- Avoid lines that attack a group for religion, ethnicity, gender, sexuality or disability, and lines about a real tragedy.

DELIVERABLE (final message, under 1,000 words)
1. FORMS: one line each with link, views, date, recipe and how earnestly it plays.
2. LINES: one line each with the exact words, source link and proof people repeat it.
3. PITCHES: exactly 8, ranked: a title, FORM plus LINE with links, the first 2 seconds, the idea in 2 sentences, and why someone sends it.
4. METHOD LOG: 5 to 8 lines on what found your best material, the dead ends, and the vidIQ credits spent.
