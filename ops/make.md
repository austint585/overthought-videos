## Make it

1. **Research.** Every number gets a source (web search). Write `ops/episodes/<id>.md`: the idea, the script, each fact with its link, and every assumption spelled out. Austin rejected a video for unsourced assumptions and a forced question hook: no invented premises, no question nobody would ask.
2. **Script.** Our own words, 20 to 40 s narrated, `render_tap.py` markup (`*keyword*`, `13{thirteen}`, `Lives{/lˈɪvz/}` for exact phonemes). Hook readable on frame 1. When the idea comes from a reference reel Austin sent, transcribe it first (the free watch.py route above; vidIQ only if that fails) and stay close to it: same beats in the same order and the same density of slang and buzzwords, because that is where the humor is (Austin, Oct 26: our Stacy spawner script "killed all the buzz words and made it less funny"). Rewrite each line in our own words; don't lift whole sentences or the creator's catchphrase or sign-off.
3. **Build.** Everyday-object explainers use the product-studio kit: copy `pipeline/render_led.py` and `pipeline/led_scene.py` (the LED bulb episode). The look is the @speedy_devv bar: a real CC0 model from Poly Haven (`python pipeline/assets.py model <id> 2k`; textures and HDRIs the same way), a dark studio, cutaways by clipping the material (`add_clip`, no booleans on scanned meshes), a magnified section shot, a new shot every 2 to 3 s, and small top captions with one boxed keyword, leader-line labels and honesty notes. The kit runs at 30 fps and about 2 s a frame. Poly Haven skews vintage and industrial; model a modern object in code when there is no asset. Straight educational scripts are yelled (Austin's tone rule): `narrate_cb.narrate(lines, out_dir, exaggeration=1.6)`, free Chatterbox on the CPU with our synthetic Kokoro reference voice. Whisper checks every take and redoes the ones that drift; read any `narration: line N best match` warning. Satire keeps a calm, deadpan read (exaggeration 0.5, or Kokoro). Memory: Chatterbox needs about 4 GB of the laptop's commit limit, so never run it at the same time as Blender or an encode. Script length 45 to 80 s for explainers (speedy's run 75 to 85 s), "you" in almost every line, the hook a surprising true claim about something the viewer owns, the last line running back into the first so it loops. Character satire can still use the clay kit below, but code-built people are our weakest look, so make objects the cast where you can.
   Clay kit, for character satire only: Austin retired the blocky voxel look on Oct 28 ("you are also still using the same minecraft animation"); he wants the handmade clay look of the @petersindex reel in `ops/style.md`. Copy `pipeline/render_gymclay.py` and `gymclay_scene.py` (the Gym Bro apartment rebuilt in clay) or adapt an older episode's scene, and make every part with `pipeline/clay.py`: `C.boxes` in place of the voxel `boxes()`, `C.soft` and `C.ball` for props, `C.figure` for people (same pivots as the voxel figures, plus `brows` and `mouth` for expressions), `C.clay` for materials and `C.glow` for lit windows, lamps and screens. Use `prints=0.0` on big surfaces (walls, floors, counters): fingerprint ridges smaller than a pixel turn into dark blotches. Night palette like the reference: deep blue walls and world, warm key and practical lights, a blue fill, `look="AgX - Punchy"`, f-stop 1.2 to 2.2. Captions use `tap.draw_captions_clay` (cream label, Fredoka, key words in blue). Give figures varied motion (gestures, leans, hops, reaching), not the same arm swing every time, and never let two surfaces share a plane (Austin saw flicker where they did). Reuse `bkit.py`, `kit3d.py` and `sfx.py`. Look checks while the GPU is busy: `OVERTHOUGHT_CPU_PREVIEW=1 python render_<slug>.py preview ...` (Cycles on the CPU at half size, about 3 to 4 minutes a still). Full clay renders run in Eevee at about 2.8 s a frame on this laptop (a 35 s episode is about 1.6 hours), so reuse a set and keep scenes lean. Windows has a small page file: never start Blender while another render or an encode is running (on Oct 28 that ran out of commit memory and the clay shader came out magenta). Recorded sounds come from `Inputs/sonniss_gdc2026` via `sfx.py`; keep effects at least 6 dB under the voice while it talks.
4. **Pronunciation.** Run `python render_<slug>.py times` and read every `PRONOUNCE` line: words in `render_tap.LEXICON`, `{/IPA/}` overrides, and heteronyms (lives, read, live, close...), which the voice often reads the wrong way. Austin heard "Lives with his roommate" read as the plural of life, and "hypergamy" as hyper-GAY-mee (it is hy-PUR-guh-mee). For any slang or name the voice may not know, run `python -c "from kokoro_onnx.tokenizer import Tokenizer; print(Tokenizer().phonemize('word', 'en-us'))"` and fix it with `{/IPA/}` or a LEXICON entry before rendering.
5. **Preview stills first** (`python render_<slug>.py preview t1,t2,...`) and look at them: nothing blocking the subject, no stray boxes, labels readable, captions in the top third, nothing important below y=1540 or at the right edge between y=1100 and 1700. Fix and re-preview.
6. **One Blender at a time.** The laptop's GPU has 6 GB: a preview and a full render running together crashed Blender and the whole run on Oct 27. Never start a preview or a second render while a render is running; write the next episode's code meanwhile and preview it once the render and its encode have both finished (a preview during the encode ran the laptop out of RAM on Oct 27: MemoryError in the encoder). Frames are cached, so a crashed render resumes where it stopped; if one frame is a broken PNG (a few KB, from a crash mid-write), delete it so it renders again.
7. **Full render**, then QA the MP4: frames at start, middle, payoff and end; `python pipeline/qa_audio.py <mp4>` must say "no steady tones" (an electric hum bed left a buzz in the Curious George video; no hum, buzz or drone beds); loudness about -13 LUFS; 20 to 45 s; under 40 MB.
8. **Ship it** only if QA passes:
   - Copy to `videos/YYYY-M-D_Name.mp4`, delete `pipeline/out/<slug>/frames` (the disk is nearly full).
   - Commit the episode's pipeline files, the video and `ops/episodes/<id>.md` as `Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>` (message ends with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`), push, and check the raw URL answers 200.
   - Append to `ops/queue.json`: `id`, `title`, `format`, `video`, `caption` (one or two plain sentences, a tag or share prompt, 3 hashtags), `"trial": null` (a regular Reel; Austin lifted Trial-only on Oct 27), `slot` (the next free ET slot still ahead, one an hour: 10:30, 11:30, 12:30 ... 22:30. Commit and push.
